Privacy Policy — Car Parking Jam 3D

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

Car Parking Jam 3D ("the Game") is developed and published by Manjot Singh. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game on iOS and Android.

1. Local Game Data

Your level progress, unlocked 3D themes, car skins, star ratings, and coin balances are stored locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults).

2. Third-Party Advertising Partners (Google AdMob & Unity Ads)

The Game displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements served by:

  • Google AdMob (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Unity Ads (Unity Privacy Policy)

These SDKs may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS with your App Tracking Transparency consent, or Google Advertising ID on Android), coarse location, and ad interaction metrics for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and contextual or personalized advertising.

3. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue at https://github.com/manjot/Car-Parking-Jam.
