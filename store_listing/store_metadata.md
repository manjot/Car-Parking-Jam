## Full Description
Can you clear the ultimate 3D parking lot traffic jam?

**Car Parking Jam 3D: Unlimited** challenges your spatial reasoning across an infinite number of procedurally generated, 100% solvable 3D parking lot puzzles! Swipe cars, sports coupes, police cruisers, taxis, fire trucks, and buses in the right order so they can escape onto the highway without crashing into barriers or each other.

### Key Features
- **Unlimited Solvable Levels (1 to ∞)**: Every single level is generated by our reverse-dependency puzzle engine and mathematically verified to be 100% solvable. Jump to any level or conquer Boss Levels every 5 stages!
- **True 3D Isometric & Orbit Camera**: Rotate and tilt the 3D camera around the parking lot, complete with dynamic shadows, suspension tilt physics, headlight beams, and highway turns.
- **Power-Ups & Boosters**:
  - **Auto Hint**: Highlights and launches the next optimal vehicle.
  - **VIP Helicopter Lift**: Airlifts any stubborn vehicle straight over the gridlock!
  - **Lot Shuffle**: Re-aligns vehicles into a fresh solvable layout.
- **5 Unlockable 3D Worlds & Car Fleet Skins**: Play in Downtown City, Cyber Neon Night, Miami Beach Pier, Alpine Frost Peak, and Golden Sunset Dock.
